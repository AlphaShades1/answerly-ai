Answerly AI is a desktop browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. It works on Windows, Mac and Chromebooks. It does not work on iOS or Android.
It puts a small ? button on every question. Click it and you get a hint first; click Show Answer and it reveals the answer. In normal mode nothing is selected for you — you stay in control of what goes on the page. It reads the question and all of its options directly from the page, so it works with multiple choice, true/false, multiple select, dropdowns and fill-in-the-blank.
It answers every question on the page in one pass, then runs a second pass to catch anything that did not take the first time. It deliberately skips questions it cannot answer safely from text alone — essays, drag-and-drop, and anything whose answer lives in a picture or a diagram — rather than guessing. Those are the ones to use the Screenshot Tool on. If your quiz shows one question at a time, press it again on each question.
For anything the page text cannot express: diagrams, graphs, chemical structures, rendered equations, tables and image-based questions. You drag a box around the question and the AI reads the picture. You can attach up to three screenshots of the same question — useful when a passage or table sits above the question and the choices sit below it.
Stealth Mode makes the buttons invisible and selects the answer for you directly on the page, with no card and nothing visible on screen. There are two, and each one needs its parent tool switched on: Quiz Stealth requires Quiz Solver, and Screenshot Stealth requires the Screenshot Tool. The invisible buttons always sit in the top-right corner of each question — hover that corner and a small label tells you which is which.
It blocks the requests your LMS uses to log what you do during a quiz — tab-switch detection, page-view tracking and quiz event logging — across Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard and others. It covers the whole page, including quizzes that open in an embedded box. It does not hide anything from software installed on a school-managed computer, and it does not change your submitted answers or your score.
If you retake a quiz, it puts back the answers you entered last time so you do not have to re-enter every one. It only fills boxes you have left empty — anything you have already answered on the new attempt is never changed. It remembers by the answer text, so it still works when your teacher shuffles the option order. It keeps your last 40 quizzes for 60 days, and you can switch it off at any time.
Open the extension and choose Upload notes — one file, up to 7 MB, as a PDF, image or .txt. The AI then reads your own course material when it answers. It makes the biggest difference on questions where your class defines a term its own way, which is exactly the kind of question a general AI gets wrong. Your notes stay attached until you remove them.
