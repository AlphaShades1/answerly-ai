Multiple choice, true/false, multiple select (checkboxes), matching dropdowns, fill-in-the-blank, and free response. Anything built around a diagram or image is handled by the Screenshot Tool.
Canvas cannot see your browser extensions — it is a website and websites are not given that list. What Canvas does record is the shape of your attempt: when each question was viewed and answered, and whether the page lost focus. Privacy Guard blocks that logging, and Stealth Mode hides the buttons so nothing is visible on screen. Separately, proctoring software such as HonorLock or Proctorio is not a website and can see installed extensions — if your exam is proctored, that is a different situation.
